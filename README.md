# QA Interview Study Guide

An interactive study app for QA engineer interviews — drill questions with sample answers, scoring, and CSV export, right in your browser.

## How to use

Open the live page below (or open `index.html` locally). Tap any question to expand it, compare your answer against the ideal one, and track your score.

## What's inside

- Question bank across automation, API testing, manual testing, SQL, and behavioral categories, by seniority level
- Candidate answer vs. ideal answer for each question
- Common mistakes to avoid per question
- Self-scoring (0–10) with color coding
- Add, edit, and delete your own questions
- Export the full bank to CSV

## Live page

https://kmariia777.github.io/qa-interview-guide/

## A note

This is a study tool, not a cheat sheet — the sample answers are starting points. Adapt them to your real experience before interviews.
